# Meals Policy
doc_id: POL-MEALS

## 1. Daily limit
Employees travelling on company business may claim meal expenses up to **75 USD per calendar day**,
covering breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks combined. Amounts in other currencies are converted to
USD first (see POL-CURRENCY).

## 2. What is not covered
Alcohol is not reimbursable unless part of approved client entertainment. Meals already included in
a conference fee or hotel rate may not be claimed again.

## 3. Over the limit
Any amount above the daily limit is not reimbursed. The claim is still accepted; only the excess is
rejected.
