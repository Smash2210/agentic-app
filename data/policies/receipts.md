# Receipts and Duplicates Policy
doc_id: POL-RECEIPTS

## 1. When a receipt is required
A receipt is required for any expense line **over 25 USD**, measured after conversion to USD
(see POL-CURRENCY). A line of exactly 25.00 USD does not need a receipt.

## 2. Missing receipts
A line that needs a receipt and doesn't have one is not reimbursed. The rest of the claim is still
processed.

## 3. Duplicates
Two expense lines are **duplicates** when they have the same employee, the same date, the same amount
in the original currency and the same merchant. This applies within one claim and across claims the
employee has already submitted.

## 4. Handling duplicates
The first line submitted is processed normally. Any later duplicate is rejected and flagged for review.
