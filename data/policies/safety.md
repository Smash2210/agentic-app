# Travel Safety Policy
doc_id: POL-SAFETY

## 1. Trip registration
Every international trip must be registered in the travel portal before departure, so the company
can reach travellers in an emergency.

## 2. Travel insurance
The company provides travel insurance for business trips. Employees do not need to buy their own,
and private travel insurance is not reimbursed.

## 3. Emergencies
In an emergency, contact the 24-hour travel assistance line shown in the travel portal. Reasonable
extra costs caused by an emergency (for example a rebooked flight) are reimbursed with receipts.
