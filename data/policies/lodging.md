# Lodging Policy
doc_id: POL-LODGING

## 1. Nightly cap
Hotel and other lodging costs may be claimed up to a cap **per night**, by the city of the stay:

| City | Cap per night (USD) |
|---|---|
| Berlin | 180 |
| London | 220 |
| Any other city | 140 |

Amounts in other currencies are converted to USD first (see POL-CURRENCY).

## 2. How the cap applies
The cap applies to each night on its own. A cheaper night does not make room for a more expensive one,
and nights may not be averaged across the stay. Taxes and mandatory city fees count towards the cap.

## 3. Over the cap
Any amount above the nightly cap is not reimbursed. The claim is still accepted; only the excess is
rejected.

## 4. What is not covered
Minibar, room service, laundry and in-room entertainment are not lodging. Room-service meals fall under
POL-MEALS; everything else on that list is not reimbursable.
