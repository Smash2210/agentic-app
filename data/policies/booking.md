# Travel Booking Policy
doc_id: POL-BOOKING

## 1. How to book
All flights, trains and hotels are booked through the company travel portal. Bookings made
elsewhere are reimbursed only if the portal could not offer the trip, and the employee must say
why on the claim.

## 2. When to book
Flights should be booked at least 14 days before departure. Later bookings are allowed for urgent
business, but the reason must be given.

## 3. Class of travel
Economy class is the standard for all flights. Business class is allowed only for a single flight
segment longer than 8 hours, with written approval from a vice president before booking.
Premium economy and seat upgrades paid by the employee are not reimbursed.

## 4. Rail instead of air
For journeys under 4 hours by train, rail is preferred over flying. Standard class is the default.
