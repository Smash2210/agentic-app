# Conferences and Training Policy
doc_id: POL-CONFERENCE

## 1. Registration
Conference and training fees are booked through the company travel portal and paid by the company
directly where possible.

## 2. Meals included in the fee
Meals included in a conference or training fee may not be claimed again as meal expenses
(POL-MEALS §2). Meals outside the programme follow the normal meals policy.

## 3. Approval
Attending a conference needs manager approval before registering.
