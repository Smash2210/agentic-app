# Client Entertainment Policy
doc_id: POL-ENTERTAIN

## 1. Approval
Client entertainment must be approved by the employee's manager before it takes place.

## 2. Limit
Client entertainment is reimbursed up to 100 USD per attendee, employees included. The claim must
list every attendee, their company and the business purpose.

## 3. Alcohol
Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of approved client entertainment, and only in moderation.
