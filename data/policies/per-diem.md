# Per Diem (Incidentals) Policy
doc_id: POL-PERDIEM

## 1. Daily rate
Employees travelling on company business receive a daily allowance for incidentals (tips, small
local transport, phone, laundry), by the trip's destination city:

| City | Rate per day (USD) |
|---|---|
| Berlin | 60 |
| London | 70 |
| Any other city | 45 |

No receipts are needed for the per diem. It is paid on top of meals (POL-MEALS) and lodging
(POL-LODGING), not instead of them.

## 2. Travel days
The first and the last day of a trip are paid at **75%** of the daily rate. Every full day in between
is paid at 100%.

A trip counts calendar days from the start date to the end date, both included. A trip from
10 March to 13 March is four days: 10th (75%), 11th (100%), 12th (100%), 13th (75%).

## 3. Same-day trips
A trip that starts and ends on the same calendar day counts as **one day at 75%**.

## 4. Rounding
Per diem amounts are rounded half-up to 2 decimals (see POL-CURRENCY section 3).
