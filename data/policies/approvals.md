# Claim Submission and Approval Policy
doc_id: POL-APPROVALS

## 1. Deadline
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Late claims need written
approval from the finance team.

## 2. Who approves
Every claim is approved by the employee's line manager. Claims with a total over 2,000 USD also need
approval from a director.

## 3. Conflicts of interest
Nobody may approve their own claim, or a claim for a trip they took part in.

## 4. Payment
Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll, in USD.
