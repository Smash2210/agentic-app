# Ground Transport Policy
doc_id: POL-TRANSPORT

## 1. Public transport first
Use public transport where it is practical. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed for airport
transfers, travel after 21:00, travel with heavy equipment, or when public transport would take
more than twice as long.

## 2. Car rental
Rent a compact car or smaller unless three or more employees share it. Fuel, parking and tolls
during business use are reimbursable with receipts. Fines and penalties are never reimbursed.

## 3. Private car mileage
Using a private car for business is reimbursed at 0.40 USD per kilometre, instead of fuel. The claim
must state the date, start, destination and kilometres driven.
