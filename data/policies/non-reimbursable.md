# Non-Reimbursable Expenses
doc_id: POL-NONREIMB

## 1. Personal expenses
Personal items are never reimbursed: clothing, toiletries, personal entertainment such as films
or spa treatments, and travel for family members or companions.

## 2. Fines and penalties
Traffic, parking and other fines, and late-cancellation fees caused by the employee, are not
reimbursed.

## 3. Covered elsewhere
Phone calls, mobile data, tips not on a meal bill and small local transport are covered by the per diem
(POL-PERDIEM §1) and may not be claimed separately. A service charge or tip on a meal bill is part of the meal.
